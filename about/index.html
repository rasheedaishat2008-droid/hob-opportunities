About HOB Opportunities

A discovery platform connecting people and organisations with opportunities for learning, funding, research and professional growth.

A platform by Hands on Blue

HOB Opportunities is a platform by Hands on Blue Ocean Conservation Trust, a Nigerian conservation organisation. The directory is designed to make opportunity information easier to discover and compare across disciplines, not only environmental work.

How listings are handled

Listings should be checked against official provider information. Eligibility, benefits, documents and deadlines can change. Applicants should always consult the official announcement and application portal before submitting an application.

Corrections and submissions

Organisations and readers can submit opportunities or report errors. Submissions are reviewed before publication. A listing on this platform is not an endorsement or guarantee of selection.
