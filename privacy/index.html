Privacy notice

How this website handles saved items and opportunity submissions.

Saved opportunities

The saved-opportunities feature stores IDs in your browser's local storage. It does not require an account. Clearing browser data may remove saved items.

Opportunity submissions

If the submission form is connected, the information you provide will be sent to HOB for review. Do not include passwords, financial details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information.

Third-party links

Opportunity pages may link to external websites. Those websites operate under their own privacy and data-handling policies.

Before launch

HOB should update this notice with the final contact address, retention period, form processing details and any analytics or anti-spam services actually enabled.
